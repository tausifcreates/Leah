# Leah vs Infuse on the Mac

Infuse, by Firecore, is a media player and library app for Apple devices. It builds a library with artwork from your Mac, network shares and media servers such as Plex, and syncs it across iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro. Leah is a video player only for the Mac: you open a file and it plays, with fast seeking and no library.

## Choose Leah if

- You play video files from your Mac and want them to open straight away, without building a library first.
- You want seeking and scrubbing that keep up with you: skip, hold an arrow key or drag along the timeline, and the picture follows. See [Fast seeking](fast-seeking-video-player-mac).
- You want to pay once, with no subscription, and get lossless Dolby TrueHD in up to 7.1 channels.
- You want an app that never connects to the internet and collects no data.

## Choose Infuse if

- You want a library of your movies and shows with posters, descriptions and watched history.
- You stream from a NAS, network shares, Plex, Emby, Jellyfin or cloud storage.
- You watch on an Apple TV, iPhone or iPad as well as your Mac, and want your library and progress to follow you.
- You need Picture in Picture or DTS-HD Master Audio in full.

## Formats

Leah plays MP4, MOV and M4V, and MKV and WebM files with H.264, HEVC, MPEG-2, MPEG-1, VP9 and AV1 video, Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, DTS, AAC, FLAC, Opus, Apple Lossless, PCM, MP3 and MP2 audio, and SRT, ASS/SSA, WebVTT, PGS and VobSub subtitles. See [Formats Leah plays](formats).

Infuse's list is wider, and also includes VP8, H.266 and disc folders such as BDMV and VIDEO_TS. Some of it, such as lossless audio, needs Infuse Pro.
