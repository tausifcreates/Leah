# Leah vs Elmedia Player

Elmedia Player, by Eltima (Electronic Team), is a video player for the Mac with a free version and a PRO upgrade that adds streaming to Apple TV, Chromecast and DLNA devices, Picture in Picture and more. Leah is a video player for Apple silicon Macs, made to be simple and to seek instantly, with everything included for one price.

## At a glance

| | Leah | Elmedia Player |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $9.99, once | Free, with PRO from $19.95 on Eltima's site or $19.99 on the Mac App Store |
| Where to get it | Mac App Store | Eltima's site, the Mac App Store or Homebrew |
| Macs | Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later | Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 10.13 or later |
| MKV and WebM | Yes | MKV, yes |
| Dolby TrueHD | Up to 7.1, exactly as recorded | Not listed by Eltima |
| DTS | Yes, the 5.1 core | Yes |
| Blu-ray PGS subtitles | Yes | Not listed by Eltima |
| DVD VobSub subtitles | Yes | Yes |
| AV1 video | On Macs with Apple M3 or later | Yes |
| Timeline preview | Yes | Yes |
| Playlists | No | Yes |
| Picture in Picture | No | PRO |
| Streaming to Apple TV, Chromecast and DLNA | No | PRO |
| Online subtitle search | No | Yes |
| Internet access | Never connects | Searches for subtitles online, and streams with PRO |
| Data collected | None | None, according to its App Store listing |

## Choose Leah if

- You want seeking and scrubbing that keep up with you: skip, hold an arrow key or drag along the timeline, and the picture follows. See [Fast seeking](fast-seeking-video-player-mac).
- You play Blu-ray rips and want lossless Dolby TrueHD in up to 7.1 channels and PGS subtitles.
- You want one low price with every feature included.
- You want an app that never connects to the internet.

## Choose Elmedia Player if

- You have an Intel Mac, or a Mac with a macOS version older than 15.
- You want to stream videos to an Apple TV, a Chromecast or a DLNA TV.
- You want playlists, Picture in Picture, an equalizer or A-B loops.
- You want to search for and download subtitles from inside the player.

## Formats

Leah plays MP4, MOV and M4V, and MKV and WebM files with H.264, HEVC, MPEG-2, MPEG-1, VP9 and AV1 video, Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, DTS, AAC, FLAC, Opus, Apple Lossless, PCM, MP3 and MP2 audio, and SRT, ASS/SSA, WebVTT, PGS and VobSub subtitles. See [Formats Leah plays](formats).
