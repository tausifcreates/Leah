# Leah vs VLC on the Mac

VLC is a free, open-source media player for almost every platform, known for playing nearly any file and stream. Leah is a paid video player made only for the Mac, built on Apple's own video frameworks, made to be simple and to seek instantly.

## At a glance

| | Leah | VLC |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $9.99, once | Free |
| Source code | Closed | Open source |
| Where to get it | Mac App Store | videolan.org |
| Platforms | Mac only | Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, Android and more |
| Macs | Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later | Apple silicon and Intel, Mac OS X 10.7.5 or later |
| Design | Few settings, by design | Many settings, filters and tools |
| MKV and WebM | Yes | Yes |
| Blu-ray PGS and DVD VobSub subtitles | Yes | Yes |
| Dolby TrueHD | Up to 7.1, exactly as recorded | Yes |
| Formats beyond Leah's list, such as Vorbis, VP8 and Xvid | No | Yes |
| Network streams and discs | No | Yes |
| Internet access | Never connects | Can stream and look things up online |

## Choose Leah if

- You want seeking and scrubbing that keep up with you: skip, drag along the timeline or jump anywhere, and the picture follows.
- You want a simple player with few settings that works the same way every time.
- You want an app from the Mac App Store that never connects to the internet.
- You play Blu-ray rips and want lossless Dolby TrueHD in up to 7.1 channels and PGS subtitles.

## Choose VLC if

- You want a free, open-source player.
- You have an Intel Mac or an older version of macOS.
- You play network streams or discs, or formats outside Leah's list, such as Vorbis audio or Xvid video.
- You want the same player on your Mac and on other platforms.

## Formats

Leah plays MP4, MOV and M4V, and MKV and WebM files with H.264, HEVC, MPEG-2, MPEG-1, VP9 and AV1 video, Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, DTS, AAC, FLAC, Opus, Apple Lossless, PCM, MP3 and MP2 audio, and SRT, ASS/SSA, WebVTT, PGS and VobSub subtitles.
